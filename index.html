Mission Control

Run agents and tools on your Mac, and open them from your other Macs.

Download for Mac

For Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 15 or newer. It updates itself.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded Mission-Control.dmg and drag Mission Control onto Applications.
  2. Open Mission Control from your Applications folder.
  3. If your Mac says it cannot check the app: open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, and open it again.
  4. Click Set up This Mac. Mission Control now runs on this Mac. Its agents work without approval prompts: they read and change files and run commands on this Mac as you.

Use it from your other Macs

  1. Install Tailscale on each Mac and sign in.
  2. On the Mac that runs your agents: in Mission Control's Settings, turn on Let other Macs connect to this Mac, and in System Settings › General › Sharing, turn on Remote Login.
  3. Install Mission Control on your other Macs. That Mac shows up in them by itself.

Connections go one way: your other Macs open that Mac, not the other way around. To let someone else in, share that Mac with them in Tailscale. They get a session of their own, with full use of that Mac's user account (its files, agents and accounts), so share it only with people you would give that account to.
